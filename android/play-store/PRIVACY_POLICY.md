# Privacy Policy for Hyperion MARINA Reviewer

**Last updated:** September 2026

Hyperion MARINA Reviewer ("the Application", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy governs your use of the Hyperion mobile and web application.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Usage
Hyperion is an offline-first educational reviewer application designed for maritime licensure preparation.
- **We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information (PII).**
- **We do NOT collect location data, device identifiers, biometric information, or contacts.**
- **We do NOT track user behavior, analytics, or telemetry.**

### 2. On-Device Local Storage
All your exam records, study sessions, question bookmarks, flags, notes, custom imported question banks, and daily goals are stored exclusively on your device's local database (IndexedDB / SQLite / Room).
- Your data never leaves your physical device unless you explicitly use the "Export Backup" feature to save a backup file to your own storage or cloud drive.
- You have complete control to wipe or reset your progress at any time through the in-app Settings / Administration screen.

### 3. Network and Internet Access
The Application only requires network access when:
- You download the initial application assets or updates.
- You choose to share an exported backup file through third-party sharing intents.
The core functionality of studying, answering questions, running mock simulations, scoring, and bookmarking functions completely offline without any internet connection.

### 4. Third-Party Services and Advertising
- The Application contains **NO third-party advertisements**.
- The Application contains **NO third-party tracking SDKs** or analytics frameworks.

### 5. Children's Privacy
The Application is designed for adult maritime students, cadets, and deck officers preparing for professional licensure. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any revisions will be reflected in the updated policy document within the Application or our official repository.

### 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the development team through our application support portal or GitHub repository.
